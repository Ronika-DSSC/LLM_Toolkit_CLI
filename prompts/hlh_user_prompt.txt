	  - Malignancies: Especially hematologic cancers (lymphoma, leukemia) including
	  - Autoimmune/Rheumatologic diseases: Systemic JIA, lupus, Still’s disease. [chop.edu]
        * Previous Episodes of Hyperinflammation
          - Sepsis-like presentations without clear source.
	  - Cytopenias or unexplained hepatitis in prior hospitalizations. [litfl.com]
        * Concurrent Diagnoses
          - Any condition noted within 6 months of HLH onset should be documented separately (Concurrent Diagnosis CRF), while older diagnoses go under Past Medical History CRF

  * the dates of irritability, and whether it is normal or abnormal.  Irritability is the quality or state of being irritable.  Examples are annoyed, angry, and irritable; an infant crying during an exam is normal.

- If information is missing, use null.
- Make every attempt to ensure the lists of json objects with non-trivial, correct information.
